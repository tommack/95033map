Branches must leave each junction in the real order, and lines mustn't cross unless the real roads do.

Click a node or line for its options: pin, hide a tail, map exit, branch, major road, color, label, bends.

Drag nodes to arrange. A dragged node is pinned (●) and Straighten leaves it alone. P toggles pin.

Drags snap to 45° (or 22.5°, under Angles) lines through neighbors, then to the grid. Hold Alt to drag freely.

Double-click a line to add a bend (it lands on the road's real turn). Double-click a bend or press Delete to remove it.

Select area (or Shift-drag) to select a region, then drag the shaded area to move it. Shift-click adds or removes one. Drag empty space to pan, scroll to zoom. ⌘Z undo.
